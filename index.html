Rule 1: Percentage Increase / Decrease Comparison

If salary of Person A is x% more than Person B, then Person B's salary is less than A by:

Shortcut = x100 + x × 100 %

Example Problem:

If Vishwa's salary is 25% higher than Raj's, by what percentage is Raj's salary lower than Vishwa's?

Step-by-step Solution:

Here x = 25%

Required % = 25100 + 25 × 100 = 25125 × 100 = 15 × 100 = 20%

Rule: Equal Selling Price with Same Gain & Loss %

When two items are sold at the same Selling Price (SP), one at a gain of x% and the other at a loss of x%, the seller always incurs an overall loss.

Overall Loss % = x2100 %

Example Problem:

A dealer sells two motors for ₹12,000 each. On one he gains 20% and on the other he loses 20%. Find his overall gain or loss %.

Step-by-step Solution:

Since gain % = loss % = 20% (x = 20):

Loss % = 202100 = 400100 = 4% Loss

Essential Shortcut: The EJOTY Benchmark

Memorize multiples of 5 to quickly locate any letter position in the alphabet:

E = 5
J = 10
O = 15
T = 20
Y = 25

Reverse Letter Rule (Sum of 27)

The sum of standard position and reverse position of opposite letters is ALWAYS 27.

Example: A (1) + Z (26) = 27 | B (2) + Y (25) = 27 | C (3) + X (24) = 27

Example Problem:

In a certain code language, "RAIL" is written as "18-1-9-12". How is "EXAM" written?

Step-by-step Method:

1. Identify pattern: Directly substitute alphabetical position numbers.

2. E = 5 (from EJOTY)

3. X = 24 (T is 20, X is 4 letters after T)

4. A = 1

5. M = 13 (J is 10, M is 3 letters after J)

Answer: 5-24-1-13
